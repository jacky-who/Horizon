# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely semiconductor / chip industry news for readers tracking global AI compute, NVIDIA's product and business developments, NVIDIA's China-related cooperation and policy, and the progress of China's domestic chip industry.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Structural.** New export-control regimes or major policy reversals, landmark GPU / accelerator or process-node launches from major vendors, breakthrough mass-production or yield milestones, or supply-chain shifts with broad, lasting consequences for AI compute or China's chip access.
- **7-8: Important.** Concrete product, roadmap or capacity updates, material fab / packaging investments, significant China-related licensing, cooperation or restriction decisions, credible domestic-chip tape-outs or mass-production progress, or consequential company actions backed by specific figures.
- **5-6: Useful.** Credible incremental updates, analyst reports with concrete data, regional investment or partnership announcements, or items that clarify an ongoing industry trend without changing the near-term picture.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine executive remarks, repeated coverage with no new facts, minor unconfirmed reports, shallow promotional pieces, or routine price and shipment commentary.
- **0-2: Noise.** Unverified rumors, pure stock-price commentary, clickbait, spam, or off-topic material.

# Evaluation guidance

Favour items with concrete, checkable details: product and architecture names, process nodes, memory and bandwidth figures, capacity or shipment numbers, policy provisions, effective dates, and named companies. Distinguish announced plans, vendor claims and analyst forecasts from shipped products, measured results, and enacted rules. Treat China-related cooperation, licensing and export-control news as high relevance because it directly affects both NVIDIA's China business and domestic substitution. Do not reward exaggerated headlines, and do not treat market attention or stock popularity as industry significance.

Use three to five specific topic tags. Include at least one tag that identifies the focus area so readers can tell which of the three threads an item belongs to, for example `英伟达`, `对华出口管制`, `中美芯片合作`, `国产芯片`, `先进制程`, `AI 算力`, `晶圆产能`.
