# Global chips & semiconductor profile

Use this profile when an item's primary subject is the semiconductor / chip industry: chip products and roadmaps, fabrication, packaging and process nodes, GPU and AI accelerators, memory and interconnect, supply chain, export controls, and the company or policy developments of chip makers.

Three focus areas are explicitly in scope:

- **Global vendors / NVIDIA**: new GPUs and AI accelerators, architecture and process advances, product launches and availability, foundry and packaging capacity, supply commitments, and material business developments of NVIDIA (英伟达), AMD, Intel, TSMC, Samsung, SK hynix, Micron, Qualcomm, Broadcom and similar vendors.
- **China cooperation and policy**: NVIDIA / AMD / Intel sales and licensing for China, US and allied export controls, entity-list and tariff decisions affecting semiconductors, China-US chip negotiations, joint ventures, and regulatory or diplomatic developments that change what may be shipped to or built in China.
- **Chinese domestic chip industry**: SMIC (中芯国际), Huawei Ascend (华为昇腾), CXMT (长鑫存储), YMTC (长江存储), Cambricon (寒武纪), Biren, Moore Threads, Loongson, SMEE (上海微电子), Naura (北方华创), AMEC (中微公司) and other domestic fab, equipment, materials, EDA and IP vendors — including tape-outs, mass production, yield and process milestones, capacity expansion, funding, product releases and domestic substitution.

Do not use this profile for: routine stock-price movement without an underlying chip-industry event, generic AI model or software news whose subject is not chips, macro or finance coverage that merely mentions chip equities, promotional material, or rumor-only reports. Route long-form technical deep-dives to the tech-blog profile and finance-first earnings or market coverage to finance-news.
